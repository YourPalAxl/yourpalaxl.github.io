Hello! This site now has an amazing little feature for those who wish to keep up to date with my posts here: RSS!

With some help, there are now 2 RSS feeds operational for this site! One is set up to allow you to keep up-to-date
with any posts I make in the News section of this site!
...If in the very low chance you have that amount of interest.

The second though I feel has a much better chance of your use. It's gonna be used for Callie/Online! If you've read my recent posts
then you should hopefully know what Callie/Online is, but if not, it's my webcomic project! go and check it out! Just click
the Callie/Online button in the sidebar! If the RSS is working correctly, it should notify subscribers when I release a new strip!

I have tested the RSS feeds myself and they seem to work in

  • Opera Mini 4 (Samsung SGH-E900)
  • Mozilla Thunderbird
